The philosophy

Before the program, one question: what's the limitation?

A decade across CrossFit, weightlifting, HYROX, endurance, and nutrition taught me one thing: serious athletes don't stall for lack of effort — they stall for lack of direction. I don't start with a workout. I find the one thing capping your level, and build everything around removing it.

Owning a real box keeps me honest — the same eye goes into your plan, in front of me or remote.
