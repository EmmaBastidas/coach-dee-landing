Ask me on WhatsApp
Do I need to be a competitive athlete?

No. I coach athletes chasing Semifinals and athletes chasing their first muscle-up. What matters is that you train with intent and want direction.

Can you work around my CrossFit classes?

Yes. If you're happy with your class programming, Accessory Work is built to sit on top of it. With full coaching, the plan becomes your training — and I still build it around your real schedule.

How many days per week will I train?

It depends on your assessment, your goals and your life. Most athletes train four to six days a week; the plan is built around what you can actually sustain.

How often can I send videos?

As often as the plan calls for — typically your key lifts and skills every week. I review them personally and send technical feedback directly.

How do we communicate?

Directly with me. Your program is delivered through the coaching app, and questions, feedback and adjustments go through our direct messages — not through an assistant.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Coaching is month to month. Real change takes consistency, so I ask athletes to commit to at least three months — long enough to assess, build and adjust.

What happens if I get injured?

We adjust. Coming back from injury is a specialty here — the plan is rebuilt around the limitation, not paused.

Do you coach athletes outside New Jersey?

Yes. I coach in person at my gym in Union City, NJ — minutes from Hoboken, Weehawken and Jersey City, one tunnel from Manhattan — and remotely across the U.S. and abroad. Same assessment, same plan, same eye.

Why I coach

I've spent a decade learning what actually moves an athlete.

CrossFit, weightlifting, HYROX, endurance, nutrition — I've coached across all of them, and I still coach on the floor every week. I own and run Union City CrossFit in Union City, NJ, minutes from Weehawken and Hoboken and one tunnel away from Manhattan.

That floor is where I learned the thing this whole site is built on: athletes rarely need more volume. They need the right priority. Finding it — and adjusting as you respond — is the job. I coach remotely the same way I coach in person: I look at the athlete first, then build the plan.
